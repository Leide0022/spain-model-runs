The thesis uses nine main runs: 2024, 2035 Trinity and 2035 NECP, each with three trading modes. Every main run has all our model improvements on. Side runs switch something off or try something new, to check one change or an issue.

Only two things change between the main runs: the year and power system, and the trading mode (rows marked ). Pick a run for its start line; pick a setting to read what it means.
