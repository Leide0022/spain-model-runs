Most loaded

Basemap: Natural Earth (outlines, shaded relief, public domain) and es-atlas (communities, provinces). Loading is the flow divided by the line's limit in the model. Where a corridor has several circuits, the most loaded circuit counts. Hours where the AC redispatch failed have no flows and are left out of the maximum and the hour counts.

Every real power plant in the model at its substation, coloured by fuel. Units of the same fuel at one substation are one circle; the circle's area shows the MW you pick.

Largest plants

Real vs model uses ENTSO-E's measured output per unit, matched to the model's plants in entsoe_unit_crosswalk.csv; plants ENTSO-E reports only in part are left out. Output and change are from the AC redispatch; "Redispatch change" is redispatch output minus balancing output, so it shows what the grid stage changed. Hydro includes pumped storage (pumping is not counted as output). Not shown: the model's industrial CHP, waste and mini-hydro blocks (spread over 100 substations, so not at real locations) and load shedding. Failed AC hours are left out of averages.
